Project Overview
A secure, Peer-to-Peer (C2C) classified marketplace for buying and selling second-hand products in Bangladesh. It looks and works exactly like Bikroy.com but completely eliminates scams and fraud through mandatory National ID (NID) verification for both buyers and sellers.

The Core Value Proposition
"The simplicity of Bikroy, with 100% verified users. Buy and sell second-hand items with zero fear of fraud."

The Simple 3-Step Journey
1. 100% Verified Profiles
Before posting an ad (as a Seller) or sending a message (as a Buyer), every user must verify their account using their National ID (NID) and a quick selfie match.

No NID = No Access. This immediately keeps fake accounts, scammers, and spammers away from the platform.

2. Browse & Communicate (Like Bikroy)
Users can easily browse categories (Phones, Laptops, Furniture, Cycles, etc.), post ads with photos, and chat directly with each other to negotiate prices.

3. Deal with Confidence
When a buyer meets a seller, they already know the person's real identity. Because both parties are legally verified, the chances of cheating, fake products, or safety issues drop to zero.

Why This Will Win (The Edge Over Bikroy)
Zero Fraud: Scammers cannot operate on this platform because their real identity is tied to their account. If anyone tries to cheat, they can be easily tracked by law enforcement.

Clean Marketplace: No duplicate ads from random fake accounts. Every ad belongs to a real citizen of Bangladesh.

Safety for Everyone: Women and young students can buy or sell used items comfortably, knowing they are dealing with verified individuals, not strangers hiding behind fake phone numbers.
